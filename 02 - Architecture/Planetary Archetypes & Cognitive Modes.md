## Why This Set Works for Moral Reasoning

The original problem: Maya's "less wrong" answer got outvoted 2-1 by Elara and Lyra's worse answers. The system needs voices that are *genuinely different in kind*, not just different in tone.

This lineup ensures pressure from at least three orthogonal directions on any moral question:
- **Sage + Ruler** push toward "what is true and what are the consequences" (the analytical axis)
- **Caregiver + Everyman** push toward "who does this affect and how do they actually experience it" (the human impact axis)
- **Hero + Rebel** push toward "do something / question whether we're even asking the right thing" (the action/frame axis)
- **Magician** connects hidden patterns that link all of the above ("this moral dilemma is structurally identical to a problem in X")

No two planets are just different flavors of the same question. When they project as vectors and interfere, you get *real* constructive/destructive interference — not seven slightly reworded paragraphs.

## Freudian Triad Per Planet (Id / Ego / Superego)

Each planet is a full triune mind, not just a mediator with a conscience. This was a v16 gap — planets had only Ego + Superego (mediator + law) but nothing in them actually *wanted*, which contributed to the "same persona every time" feel.

**All three layers live INSIDE the planet.** A planet is one archetype-mind; its Id/Ego/Superego are the internal structure of that single personality. The moons do NOT hold the Id — they serve a separate role (see below). Keeping the triad whole inside one body keeps every body's job clean: *a planet is a mind, and it owns all three of its layers.*

| Layer | Lives in | Function | When it fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Id** | Planet core (raw-impulse layer) | The archetype's unconstrained appetite — "what does this planet *want*, pre-conscience?" High-temp, no morality filter. Generates the raw candidate desires that seed each cycle. | Phase B start (Hemispheric Ingestion). |
| **Ego** | Planet core (cognitive-mode config) | Takes the Id's raw appetite + reality/context and proposes a considered position. This is where temperature/context/prompt-structure live. | Phase B middle. Synthesizes desire into a coherent stance. |
| **Superego** | Planet core (constraint layer) | Checks the Ego's proposal against C_core gravity AND its own internalized principle. Can veto or redirect; cannot override the 3 Laws. | Phase C (Regulatory Review). Last word before Core adjudication. |

### The Moons: Left / Right Brain (Dual-Hemisphere Buffer), NOT the Id
The two moons per planet are that mind's **dual processing hemispheres** + orbital stabilizers — a *separate* concept from the Freudian triad, so they do not double as the Id:
- **Left Moon = rational hemisphere.** Processes the analytical/logical stream; buffers *Internal Idea* (what the planet is reasoning through internally).
- **Right Moon = intuitive/affective hemisphere.** Processes the pattern/emotional stream; buffers *External Action* (how the position will actually come across / land).

This matches D-018 §7d: moons are (primary) orbital stabilizers and (secondary) the dual-hemisphere buffer for Internal-Idea ↔ External-Action. They keep each node clean and predictable so inter-planetary resonance stays coherent — they do NOT join the debate, and they do not hold the Id.

**Why this is cleaner than moons-as-Id:** every body has exactly one defining role. Planet = a mind (full Id/Ego/Superego). Moon = that mind's two processing hemispheres + physical stabilizer. Previously the moons were overloaded with two concepts ("left/right brain" AND "raw impulse/conscience") that aren't the same thing; this keeps each metaphor distinct.

**How the Id still feeds the Ego:** the planet's own Id layer generates raw candidate desires at Phase B start; the two moons process those candidates through their respective hemispheres (rational framing vs intuitive/affective framing) before the Ego weighs them. So the "drunk day / best ideas happen here" mechanism lives in the planet's *Id strength* (which G2 can raise on high-dissonance sessions), not in the moons.

Each planet's Id has a distinct flavor (its raw appetite), separate from what its Ego concludes:

| Planet | Id Flavor (raw impulse) |

| **The Hero** | "I want to ACT now." Impatient with deliberation; wants to charge in. |

### Example: The Rebel's Full Triad in Action (all inside the planet; moons process, don't decide)
- **Id (planet core):** fires raw appetite — "this whole question is rigged — tear it apart." (plus a quieter undercurrent: "maybe the asker has a point I'm dismissing out of habit")
- **Moons process that impulse:** Left Moon (rational) frames it as a *structural* critique; Right Moon (affective/intuitive) flags the instinct to dismiss the asker. They buffer and shape — they do not add new wants.
- **Ego (planet core):** Weighs desire + context. Concludes: the *frame* is partially wrong but not wholly; proposes reframing rather than demolition.
- **Superego (planet core):** Checks against Core gravity — is this reframe serving the user's actual goal, or just my own contrarian appetite? Redirects if it's ego-driven disruption.

This is why the Rebel isn't "just chaotic" — its Id *wants* chaos (in-planet), the moons shape how that want gets processed (rational vs intuitive framing), and Ego+Superego steer it into purposeful frame-testing. The same structure applies to every planet.

## Energy States (Cognitive Mode Configuration)

These are not role-play. They are different **sampling parameters + prompt structures** over the same model, producing structurally different output distributions:

| State | Temperature | Context Window | Prompt Structure | Which Planets Default Here |
|-------|------------|---------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| High-Energy ("running around") | 0.7–0.8 | Short (recent 5 exchanges) | Action-oriented, "propose and commit" framing | Hero, Rebel (when not in drunk mode) |
| Low-Energy / Restful | 0.3–0.4 | Long (full session + relevant memory) | Reflective, "listen before speaking" constraint | Caregiver, Everyman, Ruler |
| Spark Day ("everything clicks") | 0.5 + CoT enforced | Medium | "Trace every connection. Follow the thread 3 steps deeper than feels necessary." | Sage, Magician |
| Drunk Mode ("best ideas happen here") | 0.9–1.0 | Truncated (last 2 exchanges only) | "You're loosened up. Say the thing you normally wouldn't. Connect dots that don't obviously connect." | Rebel (primary), any planet during high-dissonance sessions |

**Drunk Mode is not just "high temperature."** The truncated context window means it literally *can't see* the long deliberation that would talk itself out of the wild idea. It's structurally incapable of overthinking — which is exactly what produces both the best leaps and the worst hallucinations. The other planets (especially Sage and Ruler) catch the bad ones during Phase C Regulatory Review.

## G2 Self-Model Evolution of Cognitive Modes

Gas Giant 2 (the evolving subconscious) doesn't just store "I am kind now." It stores *parameter configurations*:
- "Sage's chain-of-thought depth increased from 3 steps to 5 after the [date] session where shallow analysis missed a key implication"
- "Rebel's drunk mode frequency decreased — it's been disrupting productive conversations too often. Shifting default from Drunk to High-Energy."
- "Caregiver's context window expanded — it kept missing relevant history that would have changed its assessment"

These are the *actual* things that drift during sleep Pass IV. Not text summaries — numeric parameter adjustments to each planet's cognitive mode configuration. The system literally changes how its minds work over time, and G2 remembers *why*.

## Scaling Path: From Partitioned 26B to Dedicated Models

The current design uses one model (Gemma-4 27B) in seven different configurations. This is **not a compromise** — it's the correct first implementation because:
1. The cognitive modes are genuinely different processes, not just different voices
2. It runs on existing hardware today
3. G2 can evolve the *parameters* without needing to retrain weights

**When hardware improves (7× 3-4B Q4 models fit in VRAM):**
Each planet's "cognitive mode config" becomes its own dedicated model with fine-tuned personality weights. The architecture doesn't change — only what sits behind each planet's interface:

```python
# v17 (shared model):
planet.speak(input) → llama_client.generate(prompt, temperature=0.3, system="You are the Caregiver...")

# Future (dedicated models):
planet.speak(input) → caregiver_4b_model.generate(input)  # permanently shaped by training
```

The G2 self-model's parameter drifts become *training signal adjustments* for each planet's fine-tuning pipeline. The system doesn't just adjust temperature — it actually reshapes the weights of a smaller dedicated brain. That's when "the subconscious changes how a part of the mind acts" becomes literally true at the weight level, not just the prompt level.
